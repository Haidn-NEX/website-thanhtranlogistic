About the Developer
Thanh Tran Logistics
Company Limited
Developer of AIO Kids App

Thanh Tran Logistics Company Limited is a Vietnam-based technology and logistics company specializing in wholesale fashion supply chain solutions for children's apparel. We built AIO Kids to simplify the ordering process for retailers, shop owners, and distributors across the region.

Our mission is to make children's fashion wholesale accessible, transparent, and efficient — empowering small businesses and entrepreneurs to compete and grow in today's market.
